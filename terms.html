Personal-use notice

Kevin Memory Drive Sync is a desktop utility for the account owner’s personal use. The owner chooses the local folders, permitted file types and Google account used for the backup.

The owner retains ownership and control of their files. The sync copies allowed files to Google Drive and retains older versions in a separate backup area. Google storage limits and the availability of the owner’s computer and internet connection affect when new files can upload.

The utility uses rclone under its own software license and Google Drive under the account’s applicable Google terms. This website does not offer a paid subscription or accept payments.

Read the privacy notice for data handling and access controls. Contact the maintainer’s GitHub profile with questions.
